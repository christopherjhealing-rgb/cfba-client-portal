# AI document checker — requirements checklist (DRAFT for Chris's red pen)

Drafted from guidance notes 01/02/05/07/08-14. Mark up: add what's missing,
strike what's wrong, note per-item severity (FIR-worthy vs note-to-staff).
The checker reports each item PRESENT / MISSING / UNCLEAR with a page
reference, staff-only, and drafts FIR text for the missing items.

## Every job
**Site plan:** every boundary dimensioned · structure dimensioned AND located ·
setbacks to ALL boundaries (incl. gutter line) · existing structures shown ·
north point · street frontage · easements/sewer where relevant · scale or
"not to scale" note.
**Elevations:** every elevation showing the works · overall height from
NATURAL ground level · wall height / clearance under beam · pitch stated as a
figure · post positions dimensioned · natural ground line + FFL · materials.
**Engineering:** engineer name, company, registration no. · signed AND dated ·
dated on/after the drawing revision it certifies · wind class + terrain
category stated · site classification where design depends on it · footing
details (depth, dia, reinforcement) · tie-down and bracing · matched to THIS
structure and address · engineer's conditions reflected on the drawings.
**Cross-checks:** spans/heights/post spacing consistent between drawings and
engineering · wind class consistent across documents · one address throughout ·
all pages legible (no photos of drawings) · no missing pages in multi-page sets.

## Patio / pergola / verandah
Attachment detail (or reference) where attached · gutter + downpipe positions
and discharge · roof sheet profile · fire-separation detail where any part is
within 900 mm of a boundary · manufacturer engineering matched to site wind class.

## Shed / garage / workshop
Slab or footing detail · door/opening sizes · wall + roof cladding · open or
habitable use declared (changes class) · roller door wind rating where stated.

## Carport
Open sides shown (which ones) · setback to street boundary · fixing detail
where attached.

## Retaining wall
Structural design signed/dated · existing AND proposed levels both sides ·
retained height measured from TOP OF FOOTING · surcharge (driveway/structure/
fill) addressed · drainage behind wall + discharge point · boundary setback
and boundary-wall treatment.

## Pool / spa
Barrier line complete around the zone · gate positions + swing direction ·
heights of every barrier element (incl. boundary fences relied on) ·
non-climbable zones clear (nothing climbable inside) · pool dimensioned to
boundaries · engineering or manufacturer spec attached.

## Bushfire-prone lot
BAL assessment attached (not just a rating quoted) · by an accredited person ·
dated, for THIS lot · BAL-40/FZ: construction details on the drawings to match.

## Lodgement hygiene
Client reference sensible (no junk) · no certificates of title / BA1 forms
(flag if slipped through) · file names meaningful · site photos present?
(note existence to staff, never require).
