Getting started

Your first sign-in

Five minutes, once — then it's just a username and password.

The email this came with holds your username and a one-time setup code. Here's what to do with them, and what you'll find once you're in.

Three steps, once only
  1. 1

    Press Set up my login in the email

    It opens the portal on the First time screen with your username already filled in. (Typing the address yourself instead? Choose First time on the sign-in page.)

  2. 2

    Enter the setup code from the email

    It works once and then dies — that's what makes it safe to send. If it's expired by the time you use it, press Forgot password? and a fresh one comes to this same address.

  3. 3

    Choose your own password

    We never see it and can't read it back. From now on you sign in with just your username and that password.

What you'll see when you're in

Dashboard & My Jobs

Every job your company has with us — what's ready to download, what's with us, and anything waiting on you, each showing when it was lodged. One login covers your whole team.

Lodge a Job

Address, the structures (pick them from a list — the description writes itself), drawings and engineering — saved engineering can be ticked from My Documents. It lands straight on our board; repeating a job later is one press of Lodge Similar.

My Messages

If we need something further you'll get an email, and the request sits on the job itself. Reply and attach documents there — it files itself in front of the surveyor.

Downloads & Tools

Issued certificates as one package, re-downloadable for three months. Plus free checks under Tools — wind class, soakwells, bushfire, a site-plan drawer — that answer most questions before you'd have to ask us.

Stuck at any step? Ring 1300 029 074 and we'll do the first sign-in with you on the phone — it genuinely takes two minutes. There's also a full guide under Info Sheets once you're in.
