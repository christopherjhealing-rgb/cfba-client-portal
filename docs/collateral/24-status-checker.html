The client portal is for lodging, replying and downloading — and it needs a login. The status page is different: anyone with the paperwork can look up where an application is at. Handy for the owner, the crew on site, or a shire on the phone.

How it works
  1. 1

    Go to [STATUS PAGE ADDRESS]

    Works on any phone or computer. Nothing to install, nothing to sign into.

  2. 2

    Enter the reference number and the site address

    Both are on your CFBA acknowledgment email or paperwork — e.g. reference 12345 and 12 Example Street, Wanneroo. Spaces and dashes in the reference don't matter, and the postcode is optional.

  3. 3

    Read the timeline

    The stage the job is at, what it means, when we received it and when it last moved.

The five stages
1

RECEIVED

Loaded and queued for assessment.

2

UNDER ASSESSMENT

A building surveyor is assessing it.

3

AWAITING INFO

We've emailed you a request — assessment resumes when it's answered.

4

IN PREPARATION

Assessment done; the certificate is being prepared and signed.

5

ISSUED

Signed off — and the page tells you when it's ready to download.

"Issued" and "in your hands" are two different moments — signing happens before delivery, so the final stage says honestly where it's got to: being prepared, ready to download, or downloaded. A hold-up getting it to you? The page says that too, and our office is already onto it.
Example

Reference 12345 + 12 Example Street, Wanneroo → "Awaiting further information — we need something extra before assessment can continue. Check your email for our request. Received 22 Jun · Last activity 9 Jul." One glance, and the next move is obvious: answer the request.

Privacy, briefly

A job shows only when the reference and the site address both match — so nobody can browse other people's applications. It shows the stage, address and dates; never names, invoices or documents, which live in the client portal behind your login.
