CF BUILDING APPROVALS

CLIENT PORTAL

Your applications, all in one place.
Free for our clients

We've built a secure online portal for the builders, designers and drafters we work with. Everything you have with CF Building Approvals lives in one place — lodge a job, watch it progress in plain English, and download your certificate and stamped plans the moment they're issued. No cost, no software to install, and one login for your whole office.

What it does for you
1

Documents the moment they're issued

Your certificate and stamped plans appear automatically the second we issue them — and we email you to say they're ready. Download the lot as one file.

2

Know exactly where every job is up to

Every job shows its real status in plain English — being assessed, awaiting information, issued. Updated every few minutes.

3

Send us information without the email chain

When we need something further, the request lands on the job and in your inbox. Upload it straight back — it files itself.

4

Lodge new jobs and amendments

Submit a new application with your plans attached — it's on our board the moment you press lodge — or lodge an amendment against work we've already done.

5

One login for the whole company

Your office shares a login, or ask us for named logins for each person. Nothing to hand around when someone's on leave.

6

Your paperwork stays available

Issued jobs stay in your portal for three months after you first download them — grab another copy any time, and ask us if you ever need one re-issued.
