How to answer an information request so your job keeps moving.

Sometimes an assessment can't continue until we have something further — a missing engineering detail, a dimension, a signed document. When that happens the job pauses, and the fastest way to restart it is through the portal.

Why reply in the portal rather than by email?
  • It can't get lost. Your documents attach to the job itself, not to an inbox that's on leave.
  • It's faster. The surveyor sees it the moment it arrives, already filed against the right job.
  • Everyone can see it's done. The "with you" flag clears, so your whole team knows it's back with us.
The clock restarts when the last document arrives. Most jobs are certified within 3–4 business days of everything being received — so the sooner the request is answered, the sooner your certificate is issued. If anything in a request is unclear, reply in the portal and ask; that reaches us the same way.
