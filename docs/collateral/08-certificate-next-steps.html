You've got your certificate —
what happens next

A CDC isn't the finish line: the building permit is. Here's the last leg.

Your Certificate of Design Compliance (form BA3) says the design complies. Before work starts on site, the local government still has to issue the building permit — and that application comes from you (or your builder), not from us.

The last leg, step by step
  1. 1

    Download the full pack from the portal

    Certificate plus every stamped document, as one file. Lodge the stamped versions — the council must receive exactly what was certified.

  2. 2

    Fill in form BA1

    BA1 is the building permit application for certified jobs — the current version is under Resources in the portal, along with every other council form.

  3. 3

    Lodge with the local government, with the fee

    Most Perth councils take it online — every council's site is linked under Resources → Your local government. Lodge the BA1, our certified pack, and the permit fee together.

  4. 4

    The council issues the building permit

    For a certified application the council's own clock is 10 business days. Work can start once the permit is in hand — not before.

The three things that stall permits
  • Planning approval missing. A perfect CDC still stalls if the design needed development approval (setback variations, front-setback structures) that wasn't obtained. Unsure? Ask us before you lodge.
  • Wrong documents lodged. Unstamped or superseded drawings with the BA1 — always use the pack exactly as downloaded.
  • Other consents not attached. Building over a sewer, near an easement, or work affecting a neighbour's land can need extra consents — the request will name them, and we can point you the right way.
Design changed after certification? Don't lodge the old pack — tell us through Amend a job in the portal and we'll certify the change (BA19 at the council end). It's days, not weeks, and it beats a refused permit.
