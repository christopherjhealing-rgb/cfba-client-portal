Trial run — break it before a client can

CF Building Approvals — internal. Staff missions for testing the client portal.
August 2026 · Live system — real cards, real emails · Allow 45–60 minutes each, spread over a week

You each get a pretend building company and a set of missions. Work them as a real client would — on your own machine, some of it on your phone — and report everything that's confusing, wrong, slow or broken. Finding it now costs nothing; a client finding it costs trust.

This is the live system. Every lodgement creates a real card on the board and sends real emails. Put TEST in Your Reference and in the Notes for CFBA box on every job, so nothing gets assessed by accident — and the office deletes the cards when we're done.
Setup — the office does this first (ten minutes)
  • One test client per tester — /admin → Clients → Add a client, named ZZZ Test — [First name] (the ZZZ keeps them together and makes the cleanup sweep obvious). Email It To fills with the login email automatically.
  • Issue each tester a login to their own work email — they walk the same first-sign-in a client does, guide attached to the email and all.
  • Turn the review queue on for the trial: in Vercel set AUTO_ACCEPT_LODGEMENTS=0 and redeploy. Lodgements then wait in /admin for a human Accept/Reject — which is the only way to test rejections. Flip it back after the trial.
  • Pick addresses before you start: each tester wants one address they know is bushfire prone and one that isn't — use real lots from recent jobs; the form checks the real DFES map as you type.
How to report what you find
  • Send feedback — bottom of the portal menu, on every page. Pick Confusing / Slow / Broken / Idea, one blunt line is plenty. It captures which page and job you were on, so report from the spot where it happened.
  • Everything lands on /admin → Feedback with the weekly counts — that page is the trial's scoreboard.
  • Something stops you dead? Ring Chris. Don't work around it quietly — the workaround is the finding.
Everyone, first (ten minutes — before your missions)
  • First sign-in from the login email: setup code → choose a password. Does the email and its attached guide make sense on their own?
  • Sign out. Get your password wrong four times on purpose — the message should turn into a polite lockout with a wait and the office number, not a dead end. Use Forgot password? to finish.
  • Open the portal on your phone and click through the menu. Half our clients will only ever see it this way.

The missions — one letter each, swap if you finish early

Tester ABushfire — the shed paths

  • Lodge a steel shed at your bushfire-prone address. Within 6 m of the house + house 2016 or later → it must demand the shed's own new BAL report and refuse to lodge without it.
  • Flip the answer to before 2016 → the demand should vanish: exempt, lodge freely. Flip to Not sure → it lodges, noting we'll confirm.
  • Say the shed is 6 m or more away → no BAL anything.
  • Same shed at your non-prone address → no bushfire questions at all.

Tester BBushfire (patio) + strata

  • Patio at the prone address, within 6 m, house 2016+ → it should ask the house's rating (pick one) and require the evidence upload. Office: check the card's BAL column got stamped with what was picked.
  • The strata question is compulsory: fill everything else in and leave it unanswered — the Lodge button must stay locked and say why.
  • Answer Yes → a required Strata Plan box appears after Engineering; try to lodge without it (locked, named), then attach and lodge.

Tester CFiles — try to make a mess

  • Attach a Word doc or photo → it must be named and refused out loud, never silently dropped.
  • Two PDFs with the same filename → the second gets a -2, nothing overwritten.
  • Something over 40 MB → refused with the email-the-office path.
  • Ugly filenames — dashes, brackets, percent signs, very long — all accepted.
  • Check the "Filed as…" line, then confirm the card carries exactly those tidy site-named PDFs. Double-click Lodge — exactly one card.
  • Slip a certificate of title or BA1 into the pile — the portal leaves them out on purpose (we never need them).

Tester DDocuments, reuse, speed

  • Lodging, tick Save this engineering to My Documents and name it.
  • Second job: tick it under From Your Documents instead of uploading — the Engineering requirement should count as met.
  • My Documents page: replace the file, then delete it.
  • Open a finished job → Lodge Similar — everything but the address carries across.
  • My Details → Team: issue a second login for yourself, then disable it — the disabled one must not get in.
Tools — one pass each, any tester
  • Wind classification — a real site in, sensible class out
  • Soakwell calculator — a real roof area
  • Bushfire map check under Tools — same verdict as the lodgement form gave you
  • Site plan tool — draw one simple lot
  • Info Sheets — open two notes; do they load and read cleanly on the phone?
  • Public status checker — a test ref + address, watching it move

Everyone, during the week — the round trips (office plays its half)
Information request Office: set a test card to FIR with a FIR: note. Tester: the email arrives with the ask written out; reply on the job with a PDF → the banner flips to Answer sent — it's with us and the office gets the reply email. Office: reword the ask on the card → the amber "with you" state comes back.
Rejection Office: reject one queued lodgement with a written reason. Tester: the dashboard shows that reason word for word, with Lodge It Again.
Cancellation Tester: request a cancel from the job page. Office: the card gets CANCELLATION REQUESTED + an email; move the card to Cancelled. Tester: the job files under Past as Cancelled.
Issue & download Office: drive one test job all the way — ref, folder, assess, CDC, Issued. Tester: the ready email arrives; download the package, open every file; the page says certificates stay downloadable for 3 months and we keep a copy.
Amendment Tester: lodge an amendment against that issued job. Office: it emails whoever certified it, shows WAITING under /admin → Amendments; upload a revised certificate. Tester: both certificates sit side by side in Downloads.
Enquiry Tester: My Messages → the general thread — subject + a question. Office: answer from /admin → Enquiries. Tester: the reply lands in the portal and your inbox.
While you're in there — the things worth noticing
  • At every step, is it obvious whose move it is — yours or ours? That's the whole design; anywhere it isn't obvious is a finding.
  • Does the wording sound like us on the phone, or like a computer?
  • Anything you had to read twice, tap twice, or scroll sideways for — especially on the phone.
  • After each lodgement, answer the little portal or email? question honestly — it's the pilot's core measure and it only appears once a fortnight.
  • Send at least one piece of feedback with "Okay to ring me" ticked, so we see that path too.
The scoreboard, daily

Whoever's running the trial keeps /admin → Feedback open: the weekly counts (lodgements started vs completed, refused uploads, downloads, sign-ins, feedback) should move as the missions run — if a number doesn't move when it should, that's a finding too. The pre-flight card at the top of /admin stays green throughout.

Cleanup — before any real client is invited
  • Delete every test card from the board (search the TEST reference).
  • /admin → Clients: delete each ZZZ Test company — logins, jobs and messages cascade with it.
  • Set AUTO_ACCEPT_LODGEMENTS back (remove the 0) and redeploy.
  • Run the data-hygiene sweep in the go-live runbook (Phase 7) so the store and the pilot dials start clean.
  • One last look at /admin → Feedback: collect the findings into a list, fix or accept each one out loud, and only then send the first invitation.
The bar: a tester who hits a dead end, a silent failure, or a "what do I do now?" moment has found something a paying client would have found next week. That's the win — say it loudly, fix it quietly.
