Guidance note 08

Building on or Near a Boundary

Sheds, carports and walls close to the line — where the rules change, and what the plans must show.
The 900 mm Line

For Class 10a buildings, 900 mm to a boundary is the trigger: closer than that and fire separation applies — the parts of the structure facing the boundary need to be non-combustible or carry the required fire resistance. A steel shed usually satisfies this; timber posts, battens or cladding inside the zone usually don't. Structures clustered close to the dwelling or other buildings on the lot can also bring separation between buildings into play — we check that as part of assessment. Dimension the setback on the plan either way, so the question answers itself.

Building on the Boundary Itself
  • Planning first: boundary walls are governed by the R-Codes boundary-wall provisions for your area's R-coding — length and height limits apply, and in some cases neighbour comment through the council. Marginal? Ask us before you build it into the design — see also note 14, Planning Approval & Class 10.
  • Structure: footings stay inside the lot, clear of (or designed for) anything the neighbour has on their side, and the engineering should say the wall is designed as a boundary wall.
  • Water stays on your side: no roof drainage discharging over the boundary — show the gutter and downpipe layout.
  • Maintenance access: think about how the boundary face gets built and maintained — a zero-lot wall you can't reach is a dispute waiting to happen.
What We Need to See
  • Site plan with the setback to every boundary dimensioned — "on boundary" written where it applies.
  • Elevations showing height at the boundary.
  • Materials of any face within 900 mm of a boundary.
  • Engineering matching the site's wind class, noting boundary-wall design where relevant.
  • Planning approval, or a note that none is required and why.
The common bounce: "1 m setback" on the drawing, 850 mm once eaves and gutters are counted. Setbacks are measured to the closest part of the structure — gutters included. Dimension to the gutter line and the question never comes back.
