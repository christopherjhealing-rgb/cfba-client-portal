An FIR, worked through
  1. 1

    Set the status to FIR and send the message from the portal

    The client gets an email and sees the request against that job — not buried in a thread with the rest of their work.

  2. 2

    The client replies with the missing document

    Their reply and their file land on the card as an update, and the status moves itself to New Info Received. That automatic move is the point: an answered job stops sitting in the FIR group looking like it's still waiting on them.

  3. 3

    You get told

    An email to the office with the reply and the attachment on it, so you can see what came back without opening anything.

Watch this one on Tuesday. If a status doesn't move itself, or a reply doesn't reach the board, say so straight away — a job that goes quiet here is the failure that costs a client a week.

Your trial this week

You each get a client login as well as your staff access. Please get through this by Friday, on a test job rather than a live one.

  • Sign in for the first time with the code you're emailed
  • Set your own password, then sign out and back in
  • Lodge a job with plans attached
  • Check it appeared on the board correctly
  • Send a message about a job
  • Answer an FIR and attach a document
  • Download a package and open the zip
  • Send a general enquiry — not about a job
  • Try it on your phone, not just the desktop
  • Look at Resources — forms, wind and bushfire checkers
  • Try to break it. Wrong password, huge file, odd address
  • Reset your password and get back in
How to tell me what you find
Something's brokenTell me the same day — don't wait for Friday.
Something's clumsyWrite it down as you go. You won't remember it later.
Wording that's wrongEspecially anything a client reads.
What to includeWhat you did, what you expected, what happened. A screenshot beats a description.
Friday: fifteen minutes on what everyone found, and what's worth fixing before the first client sees it. Small things are welcome — they're the difference between a portal clients use and one they ignore.
