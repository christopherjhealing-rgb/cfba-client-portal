What runs without anyone

Nobody presses these. They matter most on the days we're too busy to notice.

Every 5 min Collects issued packages, emails clients, moves PORTAL, delivers messages
45 minutes An issued job with no files is flagged STUCK
3 days A ready package still unopened is chased
5pm weekdays The report, and the past-jobs lists rebuild
Monday 7am A digest to each client with anything outstanding
And the moment it happens
A job is lodgedCard created, files attached, client acknowledged.
A client repliesOnto the card, status off FIR, office emailed.
An amendmentEmailed to the certifier. Never on the board — over the page.
A downloadReceipt on the card, PORTAL to DOWNLOADED, retention clock starts.
Still a personChecking the autogen's Word document, PDF-ing it, and setting the status to Issued.
The 5pm report — what it's for

Everything else in the portal reacts to something happening. This is the one thing that looks for something that should have happened and didn't — the job that never reaches the client, which is the only failure here that costs somebody a week and never announces itself.

Issued, not deliveredOn the board at Issued, nothing in the portal.
Delivered, not toldThe portal has it; the email didn't send.
Ready, not openedThree days and they still haven't looked.
Amendments waitingLodged, nothing sent back — the board can't see these.
No certificateA package with everything except the CDC itself.
Lodged, not on the boardThe client believes it's with us.
It goes out at 5pm on weekdays whether there's anything in it or not, and the same items appear in Teams as they happen. A quiet report is the good outcome — but an empty inbox at 5pm means the report itself didn't run, so say something.

03

Amendments — what's changed

The only process on these six pages that works differently from last week.

Until now
  • Opened a new job on the board
  • Sat there looking like an assessment
  • Waited its turn behind real jobs
  • Took longer than doing it by email
From now
  • Never touches the board
  • Emailed to whoever certified it
  • You reply “yes” — as you do today
  • One note on the original card
How one moves
Client

Lodges it in the portal

Picks the job — including ones we finished years ago — says what's changed, attaches the new drawings.

Automatic

Emailed to whoever certified it

Off the board's Certified By column, office copied, with the reason and the documents. Nobody in that column means Rebecca.

Chris Rebecca

Reply “yes” by email

Exactly as you do today. No system, no button, nothing to learn.

Kacie

Watermark, re-issue, upload it back

The only new step for anyone — Admin → Amendments. With no card there's no SharePoint sync to collect it.

Automatic

Client emailed, and downloads it

Their original certificate stays valid until then; both sit side by side.

What it means for each of you
Kacie You stop forwarding them — the email arrives at the right person already. You still watermark and re-issue exactly as now, then upload it on the Amendments page.
Chris Rebecca Nothing changes. The request reaches you directly with the reason and the drawings attached; you reply “yes” by email as you do today.
The board Gets one note on the original card and nothing else. No new item, no status, no invoice.
The catch. Because they're off the board, the board can't notice one going quiet — the 5pm report does instead. If you get an amendment email and it isn't yours, say so rather than leaving it.

What a client can do

Their sidebar, top to bottom. Any of it switches off from Settings while we update it, without taking the portal down.

DashboardEvery job and what stage it's at, on one screen.
My JobsOne job in detail — stage timeline, who's assessing, how long it's been with us.
My MessagesA thread per job, newest first. Attachments both ways. Where an FIR is answered.
Lodge a JobAddress, class, description, PDFs — plus anything saved in My Details. Straight onto the board.
Amend a JobA change to any job we've done — current or long finished. By email, never onto the board.
DownloadsEvery package we've issued them, as often as they like.
Info SheetsThe fourteen guidance notes.
ResourcesCouncil forms, wind classification checker, bushfire map check, maps, and all thirty councils.
Site Plan ToolDraws a compliant site plan for a simple job, rather than us sending it back for one.
My DetailsContact details, and a library of documents they reuse job to job.
Help & SupportHow it works, and a general enquiry that reaches us even when it isn't about a job.

Plus: first sign-in with a one-time code · password reset · unread counts · cancel a lodged job · export their job list · works on a phone, which is where most of them lodge from. The engineering checker is built and hidden until we switch it on.

What we can do

The admin dashboard — eight places, and what each of them is for.

QueueLodgements waiting to be accepted. Normally empty — a job goes straight onto the board. Anything here means Monday was down when they pressed Lodge.
ClientsThe registry. Issue a login, email the details and one-time code, reset a password, check their SharePoint folder resolves, view the portal as them, remove a client.
EnquiriesQuestions with no job behind them; the reply goes back through the portal.
AmendmentsEvery one lodged, who it went to and why, and where the revised certificate goes back out from.
ContentThe welcome guide, info sheets, council forms and engineering sets — new versions any time, no redeploy.
SettingsWhat's connected, the evening report, Teams notifications, amendment routing, switching client pages on and off.
Turnaround ReportsHow long jobs are taking, by class, with the days spent waiting on the client taken out.
Activity LogWho did what, and when. Sync Now sits beside it, for when five minutes is too long to wait.
