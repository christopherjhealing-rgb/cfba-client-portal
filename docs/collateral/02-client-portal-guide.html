Your jobs, online

The CF Building Approvals client portal — what it is and how to get in.

Every job you have with us, in one place. Track where each one is up to, send us what we've asked for, and download your certificate and stamped plans the moment they're issued.

Lodging complete is the single biggest thing that speeds a job up. Our guidance notes set out exactly what we need for each type of work — they're all in the portal under Info sheets, and the one to read first is the lodging checklist.

What you can do
  • See every job — one login for your whole company, a plain-English status on each job, and a search box when you have dozens.
  • Lodge a new job — address, drawings, engineering; pick the structures and the description writes itself. Bushfire-prone address? The form says so as you type and asks for the BAL paperwork it needs. Repeating a job? Lodge Similar carries the work across.
  • Send us information — when we need something further, reply and attach it to the job itself.
  • Download issued jobs — certificate and all stamped documents as a single file, plus a spreadsheet export of your job list.
  • Request an amendment — on any job we've done for you, recent or years old, straight from the job's own page or Amend a Job.
  • Save the documents you reuse — insurances, licences, standard details. Attach them to a job without going and finding the file.
  • Answer your own questions — wind class, soakwells, bushfire and site plans under Tools; forms under Resources. Over the page.
Why it's worth using
  • No more chasing. The status is on screen, updated every few minutes as the job moves.
  • Nothing gets lost. What you send lands on the job, not in an inbox.
  • Faster turnaround. Requests for further information reach you straight away and go back the same way.
  • You'll still get emails. The portal doesn't replace them — an update on a job still reaches your inbox, with a link straight to it. And the moment a certificate is issued, we email you that it's ready.
  • Fewer things to send back. The tools over the page answer, before you lodge, most of what we'd otherwise have to ask you for afterwards.

How long jobs take

Most jobs are certified within about three to four business days of everything being received — the clock starts when the last document arrives, not when the job is first lodged. We don't put a date on an individual job: a certificate that has to be corrected, or that comes back from the permit authority, costs weeks, so getting it right first time is the fastest way to get you built. If a job is genuinely time-critical, tell us when you lodge it rather than after.

Changing something after we've started

Start from the job itself — Amend This Job on its page — or from Amend a Job in the menu, which also lists the jobs we finished years ago. Say what's changed and attach the new drawings. It goes straight to whoever certified the original rather than into the queue behind new work, which is why an amendment usually comes back faster than a fresh job. Progress shows on the job itself — you'll see Amended documents ready when the revised set is up — and your existing certificate stays valid until then, the two sitting side by side in Downloads. Need it stopped altogether? Request to cancel on the job's page — nothing is final until we confirm with you.

A job, start to finish — for example
Tue 9:04 You lodge a patio at 12 Example Street — structures picked, drawings and engineering attached. It's on our board a minute later, shown as lodged today.
Wed Assessment finds the tie-down detail missing. You get an email with the request written out; the job shows Awaiting information — with you.
Wed 3:10 You reply on the job with the detail attached. It files itself in front of the surveyor and the job shows Answer sent — it's with us — your whole team can see it's back with us.
Fri 11:20 Issued. The email says it's ready; Downloads has the certificate and stamped plans as one package, re-downloadable for three months.
New in the portal: a bushfire-prone lot is asked for its BAL paperwork before the job will lodge — a pre-2016 house is exempt; from 2016, a shed needs its own new report and a patio or carport the rating evidence. A strata lot takes the strata plan the same way. My Documents holds the files you reuse — tick saved engineering instead of re-uploading it. And Send feedback sits at the bottom of the menu — we read every one.

Tools we've built you

The calculators and checks live under Tools; council forms and links under Resources. All free, and none of them need us. They exist because they answer the questions we're asked most, and because most of what sends a job back is something that could have been settled before you lodged.

Wind classification Region, terrain, shielding and topography in — your AS 4055 wind class out, with the working shown. Not sure of your topographic class? It works that out too.
Soakwell calculator How much soakwell a new roof needs, and which standard sizes make it up. Adding to a lot that already has them? It sizes the whole roof, not just the new part — which is how people find out the house was never covered either.
Site plan tool Draws a compliant A4 site plan at a real scale from your actual lot boundary: north point, scale bar, dimensions to every boundary, title block. For a simple job it saves us sending it back for one.
Bushfire map check Your address straight into Landgate's bushfire-prone map. Answers "do I need a BAL assessment?" before you lodge instead of after. If you do, we can prepare it.
Patio engineering checker For supported patio systems: frame sizes checked against the engineer's published tables before you lodge. Ask us to switch it on for your account.
Council forms & your local government BA1, BA2, BA5, BA7, BA9, BA13, BA19 and BA22 kept current, and every council we deal with linked to its building application page — under Resources.
The guidance notes Fourteen of them, under Info Sheets — site plans, engineering, BAL, retaining, elevations, boundaries, pool barriers, stormwater, easements and more. The lodging checklist is the one to read first.
The three calls we get most — what wind class am I?, do I need a BAL?, and my site plan came back — are all answered there in under a minute. Ring us anyway if you'd rather; we'd just rather you weren't waiting on us for something you can settle in twenty seconds.

Frequently asked

Is it secure?
Your jobs are visible only to your logins, and that's checked on every page and every download. No file has a public link. Passwords are stored scrambled and one-way — nobody can read them back, including us.

Can more than one person use it?
Yes — share the company login, or ask us for a named login per person.

Phone or tablet?
Yes. The portal works on any device.
