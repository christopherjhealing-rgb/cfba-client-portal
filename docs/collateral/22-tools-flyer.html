For WA builders, designers and drafters

Five tools.
No charge.

Free for our clients

Most of what sends a job back to us is something that could have been settled before it was lodged. So we built the answers into the client portal — free, no software, and you don't have to ring us to use them.

The questions, and where they're answered
1
“What wind class am I?”
Wind region, terrain category, shielding and topography in — your AS 4055 classification out, with the working shown and the gust speed beside it. Not sure of your topographic class? It works that out from the landform too.
2
“How much soakwell do I need?”
Roof area in, the sizes to buy out — rounded up to a real precast well rather than left as a number. Adding to a lot that already has soakwells? It sizes the whole roof, not just the new part, which is how people find out the house was never covered either.
3
“My site plan came back again.”
Draw one properly instead. The site plan tool works from your actual lot boundary and prints A4 at a real scale — north point, scale bar, dimensions to every boundary, title block. For a simple job it saves us sending it back for one.
4
“Do I need a BAL assessment?”
Type the address and get the State's own answer, straight off Landgate's bushfire-prone map — before you lodge rather than after. And if you do need one, we prepare them.
5
“Which council form, and where does it go?”
BA1, BA2, BA5, BA7, BA9, BA13, BA19 and BA22 — Word or PDF, kept current, with a link to the official source beside each. Plus thirty councils, every one linked to its building application page rather than its home page.

And the portal they live in

Every job you have with us on one screen, in plain English. Lodge, reply, and download your certificate and stamped plans the moment they're issued. One login for your whole office, and it works on a phone — which is where most of our clients lodge from.
