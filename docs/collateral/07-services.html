One office, every approval

Certification, energy and engineering — under one roof, on one portal.

Most jobs need more than a certificate. When the pieces come from different providers they wait on each other; when they come from one office they move together. Here's everything we do — every item lodged, tracked and delivered through the same portal.

Services

Certificates of Design Compliance (BA3)

Class 10a and 10b — patios, carports, sheds, garages, pools, barriers, retaining walls, fences — plus Class 1a dwelling work. Most jobs certified within 3–4 business days of everything being received.

Amendments (BA19)

Design changed after certification? Lodge the amendment through the portal against the original job — the original certificate and its history stay intact.

Bushfire-prone lots

If a lot is in a designated bushfire prone area, a BAL assessment is required before habitable work can be certified. We don't prepare BAL reports — an accredited assessor does; the portal says what to attach at lodgement.

Energy reports

Energy efficiency assessments for Class 1a work, prepared alongside the certification so neither waits on the other.

Engineering on demand

Missing structural certification is the most common thing holding a job up. We can arrange engineering for patios, sheds and similar structures — one less contractor for you to chase.

Uncertified & shire work

We assess uncertified applications for local governments, and we know exactly what each council around Perth expects — which is why our packs go through first time.

The one-office advantage: a patio on a bushfire prone lot needs a BAL, the certificate, and often engineering. Lodge it once with us and all three move together — one portal job, one point of contact, one download at the end.
