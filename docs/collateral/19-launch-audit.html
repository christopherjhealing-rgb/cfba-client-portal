Before the first client goes on

Work top to bottom with a pen. Every box ticked once, in order, and the portal is ready. Plumbing first, then the client journey, then what we've built them — and section 6 has changed since you last read this.
1 · Environment & security
  • Vercel env vars all present: AUTH_SECRET, MAIL_FROM (proven — FIR test worked), OFFICE_EMAIL, RETENTION_MONTHS=3, Supabase keys, Monday token, Graph IDs + secret.
  • Admin passcode is set, is not "demo", and /admin signs in with it.
  • Supabase migration ran with "Success. No rows returned".
  • Supabase → Storage: the issued bucket shows Private.
  • Signed in as the test client, edit the address bar to another job's ref (/jobs/12345) — you get "not found", never someone else's job.
  • Six wrong passwords in a row — the throttle message appears and offers the reset path and phone number.
  • Graph client secret expiry noted in your calendar (24 months from creation) — it takes emails and file-pulls down silently when it lapses.
2 · The Monday loop
  • /admin shows Sync healthy, run within the last 10 minutes, without pressing anything (proves the 5-minute cron).
  • On a test card: post an update starting FIR: — it appears in the portal and the email arrives (done — working).
  • Same test with CLIENT: prefix.
  • An update without a prefix stays internal — never reaches the portal.
  • Reply from the portal — the reply lands on the Monday card as an update, and the office notification email arrives at OFFICE_EMAIL.
  • Rename a test card's client to a misspelling — it appears under Unmatched Monday clients in /admin; resolve it; jobs arrive on the next sync.
3 · Lodgement
  • Lodge a job with small PDFs — the card is on the board instantly, files sit in the Files column, and the job page shows "What you've sent us".
  • Lodge again with a big set (over ~5 MB total) — the direct-upload path, with the progress bar, succeeds and the files reach the card.
  • Try a Word document — the friendly "we can only accept PDFs" message appears and nothing is lodged.
  • Try over 40 MB — refused with the "email the biggest ones" message.

The client journey, end to end

4 · Further information round-trip
  • Set the test card to FIR — the job moves into Action required with the amber "With you since" line.
  • Reply with an attachment — it lands on the card, and the status question answers itself on the job page.
5 · Issue & download
  • Files first: certificate package into the job's SharePoint folder, then flip the card to Issued. /admin shows the green "In the issue hold" note; roughly 10–20 minutes after the flip (the 10-minute hold, plus sync cycles and OneDrive settling) the job shows Ready to download and the certificate email arrives. The hold is the undo — pull a card back off Issued inside it and the client never knows.
  • Deliberately do it wrong once: flip a second test card to Issued with an empty folder — after the hold, /admin shows the amber "Issued on Monday, but no files found" warning. Add the files; next sync delivers and emails. (This is how you'll tell if an issue "didn't work".)
  • Download the package — it opens, it's complete, every document stamped.
  • The download receipt appears on the Monday card.
  • "Download again" works, and the retention countdown shows under Downloads.
6 · Amendments — changed, do this one properly
  • Settings → Amendments: Chris and Rebecca's email addresses in, Rebecca set as the fallback. Until both are done, a lodged amendment sits silently and the client thinks it's with us.
  • Admin → Amendments → Rebuild Now. Builds each client's list of past jobs so the picker pre-populates. Rebuilds itself at 5pm daily after that.
  • Only then switch Amend a Job on in Settings.
  • Lodge an amendment against the test job. Check: no new Monday card, one note on the original card, and the email arrives with whoever is in Certified By — or Rebecca when that column is empty.
  • Upload a revised certificate on the Amendments page — the client is emailed and can download it beside the original.
  • Lodge one against a job we finished years ago, by reference — it finds the old card and notes it.

  • Wind classification checker: run one you know the answer to. Confirm the two DFES / Building and Energy links open.
  • Soakwell calculator: both halves — new wells, and adding to a lot that already has them. It sizes on Bayswater's 0.0125 throughout; decide whether that stands for Wanneroo and Joondalup or ask them.
  • Bushfire map check: an address goes through to Landgate's map.
  • Site plan tool: draw one, print it, check the scale bar and the dimensions are right on paper.
  • FIR library: red-pen the 24 requests in Admin → FIR Library. They go out over your name. Then test one live — FIR: Soil Class on a test card.

  • Resources links click-through done — especially the ones flagged // confirm in the code.

Content, notifications and go

9 · Notifications
  • Teams webhook connected and unmuted — Settings → Teams. Post a test and confirm it lands in the right channel.
  • The 5pm report arrives on a weekday, even when there's nothing in it. An empty inbox at 5pm means the report itself didn't run.
  • Monday 7am digest — check one arrives.
10 · Housekeeping, then go
  • Delete the Monday test cards; disable the test client's login in /admin.
  • Activity log (/admin/audit) shows your test downloads and actions.
  • Reports page loads and the turnaround numbers look sane.
  • Kacie and Rebecca briefed — job-flow (09) and admin guide (10) in hand.
  • REQUIRE_CDC_FILE=1 set, so a package can't go out without the certificate in it.
  • Portal on our own domain rather than the build address.
  • Privacy wording (12) signed off before anyone real signs in.
  • Incident card (11) printed and by the desk.
  • First client's welcome: login issued in /admin, first-job email (18) filled and ready to send on their next issued certificate.
  • Know the rollback: Vercel → Deployments → previous build → Promote. Sixty seconds, no code.
The one habit that matters after go-live: glance at the /admin sync banner each morning. Green and recent — everything downstream is working. Anything amber or red tells you exactly what to fix before a client notices.
