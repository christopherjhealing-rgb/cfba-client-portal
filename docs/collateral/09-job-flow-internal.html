The stages
  1. 1

    Job comes in — straight onto the board Client Automatic

    Client lodges through the portal — address, structures picked from a list (class and description write themselves), plans and engineering. The Monday card is created instantly in New Jobs — client name, email, class and description filled, the PDFs in the card's Files column, the client's note posted to Updates. Lodge Similar pre-fills a repeat job (never the address). The review queue only holds a lodgement if Monday was unreachable at that moment. Jobs still arriving by email are entered by hand as they are now.

  2. 2

    Reference number & folder Kacie

    Reference goes on the card and the SharePoint folder is created as <address> - <ref>. Until the reference exists the portal cannot find the folder, so nothing can be issued to the client.

  3. 3

    Assigned to a surveyor Kacie

    Put into the assessing surveyor's queue. Chris assesses his own jobs; Rebecca assesses hers.

  4. 4

    Assessment Rebecca Chris

    Plans and engineering assessed against NCC 2022 Volume Two and the ABCB Housing Provisions, and the checklist completed. This is where the work happens — the job stays here until the surveyor is satisfied.

  5. 5

    Further information needed → see the loop overleaf Rebecca Chris

    Only when something is needed from the client. Status set to FIR and the request written on the card's Updates tab starting with FIR: (or CLIENT: — both work). Everything else on that card stays internal.

  6. 5b

    Waiting on the engineer (FIR - ENG) Rebecca Chris

    Arranged in house — the client is shown the status so they know where the job is up to, but is never asked to supply anything or chased for it.

  7. 6

    Assessment complete — hand off to the autogen Rebecca Chris

    The surveyor sets Chris CDC (Chris's jobs) or To CDC (Rebecca's) once the assessment is finished. Rebecca's part ends here — her signature is already on the template.

  8. 7

    CDC generated automatically Automatic

    Setting the CDC status starts the script. It writes the certificate and watermarked PDFs into the job's Issued folder and moves the card to To Issue. Nobody types the certificate.

  9. 8

    Reviewed and finalised Kacie Chris

    Every document checked against the CDC: correct references, watermark, address, and that all documents are included. Edits made here are what the client receives — the portal only takes files once the job is marked Issued.

  10. 9

    Issued to the client Automatic Kacie Chris

    Sync copies the Issued folder into the portal, the job moves to Ready to download, and the portal emails the client. As it delivers it keeps the card's PORTAL column honest — ISSUED → READY → DOWNLOADED, or STUCK — which the public status page reads out. First download: receipt note on the card, and the office is emailed the job's correspondence record for the 7-year file.

  11. 10

    Invoiced Automatic Fiona

    Job closed out. Downloaded jobs stay available to the client for three months, then the stored files are purged automatically.

Three things that stop a job dead. A card with no reference number cannot be matched to its SharePoint folder, so nothing reaches the client. A card whose Client spelling matches no portal client is invisible — its jobs and messages reach nobody; the admin page lists these under Unmatched with a one-click fix. And a job set to FIR without a FIR:/CLIENT: update shows the client "further information required" with no detail and sends no email. FIR - ENG is not part of that loop — the client is informed, not asked.

When we need more information from the client
  1. 1

    Surveyor Chris

    Sets status to FIR and posts the request on the card's Updates tab beginning FIR: (or CLIENT:). This loop is for information needed from the client only. The prefix works on a card in any active status — FIR is what flags "action needed".

  2. 2

    Portal Automatic

    Next sync (≤5 minutes) imports the update, shows the job under Further information required, then emails the client the request with a link straight to the job. The email is sent by the portal, not Monday, so it can never arrive before the detail is visible.

  3. 3

    Client Client

    Signs in, sees exactly what is needed, and uploads it against the job. No email chain.

  4. 4

    Portal Automatic

    Posts the reply and files onto the card, and emails the office that a reply has arrived — Monday doesn't notify us of updates made with our own token, the email does. Files land on the card with the message.

  5. 5

    Surveyor Chris

    Picks the job back up and carries on.

What happens on its own, and what does not

Automatic

  • Monday card created at lodgement, files attached
  • CDC and stamped PDFs generated
  • Issued files copied to the portal
  • "Certificate ready" email to the client
  • Further-information email to the client
  • Office email when a client replies
  • Download receipt noted on the card
  • PORTAL column kept honest as delivery happens — the public status page reads it
  • Correspondence record emailed to the office on first download (7-year file)
  • Sync health + unmatched list on /admin; the 5pm report
  • Files purged three months after first download

Still done by us

  • Reference number and folder
  • Assigning the surveyor
  • The assessment itself
  • Writing the FIR: request
  • Reviewing and finalising the pack
  • Clicking Issued
  • Invoicing
  • Matching new client spellings (one click, /admin)

Changed after issue — the amendment loop

An amendment never opens a Monday card. The client lodges it from the job's own page (or Amend a Job); the portal emails whoever is in the card's Certified By column with the office copied — Rebecca when the column is empty — and the reply happens by email exactly as before. Kacie watermarks and re-issues as usual, then uploads the revised certificate on Admin → Amendments (no card means no SharePoint sync to collect it). The original card gets one note; the client sees progress on the job itself and both certificates side by side in Downloads. The 5pm report is what notices a quiet one — admin guide (sheet 10) has the full rules and the routing setup.
