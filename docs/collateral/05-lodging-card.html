Lodging a complete job

A complete lodgement is the single biggest thing that speeds a job up. Guidance note 07 in the portal has the detail; this card is the checklist.
Every job, every time
  • Site plan — the whole lot, the new structure dimensioned to boundaries, north point, and existing buildings shown. (The #1 reason jobs bounce — see note 01.)
  • Elevations — every relevant face, with heights and boundary relationships dimensioned. (Note 05.)
  • Engineering — signed and dated structural certification matching the drawings, with the site wind class stated. (Note 02.)
  • PDFs only, up to 40 MB per lodgement — bigger sets, email the office first.
  • Pick the structures on the form — the description writes itself; saved engineering can be ticked from My Documents. Repeating a past job? Lodge Similar carries it across.
Then add, by job type

Patio · carport (Class 10a)

✓ Connection details to the dwelling (if attached)
✓ Fire separation shown if within 900 mm of a boundary
✓ BAL rating evidence if bushfire prone — the form asks for it before lodgement

Shed · garage (Class 10a)

✓ Slab/footing detail
✓ Wind class on the engineering matching the site
✓ Boundary setbacks dimensioned (or on-boundary noted)
✓ New BAL report if bushfire prone and the house is 2016+ (older: exempt)

Pool · spa + barrier (Class 10b)

✓ Barrier line marked on the site plan
✓ Gate positions and swing direction
✓ Non-climbable zones achievable — fence heights shown
✓ Pool + barrier engineering/specification

Retaining wall (Class 10b)

✓ Levels both sides of the wall
✓ Drainage behind the wall shown
✓ Engineering for the retained height
✓ Distance to boundaries and structures (Note 04.)

Before you press lodge
  • Is the address exactly right — including lot number and suburb?
  • Do the drawings, engineering and address all refer to the same job and revision?
  • Bushfire prone? The form checks the map. A pre-2016 house is exempt; from 2016 a shed needs its own new report, a patio/carport the rating evidence — the form won't lodge without it. We can prepare a report.
  • Strata? The form asks Yes or No — a Yes makes the strata plan a required upload.
  • Does the design meet R-Codes setbacks, or do you have planning approval? A compliant certificate still stalls at the council if planning isn't settled.
Not sure something's needed? Lodge with a note in the "Notes for CFBA" box — it goes straight to the assessing surveyor — or message us on any job. Asking first is always faster than a bounced application.
