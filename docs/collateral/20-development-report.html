Behind the counter, and what's next

Operations (the office view)
  • Five-minute auto-sync with a health banner — one glance covers the whole system
  • Ten-minute issue hold — an undo window before any client is told
  • OneDrive guard — never ships a half-synced folder; Word templates auto-excluded
  • Unmatched-client workbench; issued-but-no-files and email-failure warnings
  • Admin toggles: hide any section live; supersede info sheets without a deploy
  • Council-forms manager; engineering-set manager with per-client access
  • Named client logins; append-only audit log; turnaround reports vs the published 3–4 days
  • Weekly client digest (optional switch)
Security & compliance
  • All documents in private Australian storage; every download ownership-checked
  • Hardened sign-in: hashed passwords, one-time setup codes, attempt throttling
  • Silent-failure class eliminated — every write verified, every failure visible
  • Data minimisation: declined-lodgement files deleted; 3-month purge honoured
  • WA public holidays (2026–27, verified) in the turnaround maths
  • Privacy notice drafted; collection wording ready for sign-off
Marketing & collateral (19 documents)
  • Client flyer with QR, two-page client guide, welcome letter
  • Launch, login and certificate-ready email templates
  • Services one-pager; certificate next-steps sheet
  • Internal job-flow guide, admin guide, incident card
  • Two-page go-live audit checklist
  • Full print system matching the portal brand
Specced and queued (designs agreed, in the register)
  • Address autocomplete — real addresses picked from a list, no typos
  • AI document checker — every Class 10 lodgement checked against our requirements; staff-only findings + a drafted FIR
  • Guidance assistant — answers from our own published notes, hands off to messages
  • Site plan tool v2 — draw over the real cadastral lot & aerial by address; generated elevations
  • One-off public lodgement — no-login homeowner jobs via magic links
The shape of the week: the portal now lodges faster than email, answers "where's my job?" before the phone rings, carries our guidance library, and drafts the client's site plan. Next: it starts checking the documents too.
